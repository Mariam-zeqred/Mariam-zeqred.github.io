Privacy policy for "mailer"

Last updated: 20 October 2026

"mailer" is a personal, single-user tool written by and for Mariam Loutfi. It helps her prepare and send her own application emails from her own Gmail account. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope gmail.modify), the tool creates email drafts in the owner's Gmail account, reads the labels on those drafts, and sends them. It does not read, search or store other messages in the mailbox.

Where data is stored

The OAuth token and a small record of which drafts were created and sent are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's Gmail API. No data is shared with, sold to, or made available to any third party.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token file, after which the tool has no access.
