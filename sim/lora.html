A calculator, not a measurement. The log-distance exponent n (2 free space, about 2.7–3.5 in open rural or suburban terrain, 4 and above with obstructions) dominates the answer, and real links vary by tens of dB with terrain, vegetation and antenna placement. Treat the range as an order of magnitude and confirm it with a field test; the radio horizon is a separate hard limit.

Formulas

Sensitivity = −174 dBm/Hz + 10·log₁₀(BW) + NF + SNRreq(SF), with the required SNR from −7.5 dB at SF7 to −20 dB at SF12. Link budget = Ptx + Gtx + Grx − sensitivity. Path loss = free-space loss at d₀ + 10·n·log₁₀(d/d₀). Range is the distance where the received power falls to sensitivity + fade margin. Symbol time = 2SF/BW; the airtime uses the SX127x formula with explicit header, coding rate 4/5, 8 preamble symbols and low-data-rate optimisation when the symbol exceeds 16 ms.
